## Unreleased

## 0.1.0

- Initial Windows release: local SQLite clipboard history, exact deduplication,
  full-text search, full plain-text preview, and keyboard navigation.
- Enter pastes plain text; Shift+Enter offers saved HTML and RTF; Ctrl+Enter copies
  plain text without pasting. Existing plain-text history migrates in place.
- Tray capture controls, a gray clipboard icon, and a compact result list with
  alternating rows and no timestamps or page count.
- Self-contained ZIPs for Windows x64 and Arm64; no separate .NET installation.
